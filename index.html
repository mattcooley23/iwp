THE FLAT-FEE DIFFERENCE

We don't believe the cost of financial advice should be solely predicated on how much money you have.

 INDUSTRY'S STANDARD
Under the industry's most common way of billing, your fee often increases when:
You save well
You receive an inheritance
You roll over a 401(k)
The market rises
You have vesting stock
OUR STANDARD
ONE FLAT FEE
You're never penalized for good things happening.
Instead, we believe the cost of financial advice should be a flat fee based on the unique complexity of your financial life, not a toll on every next dollar you earn.
